# Game length

**Question.** Are the games in the feed short loops made for it, or can any game appear?

**Status: open.**

## Options

**Short loops, with a round under a minute.** This matches a swipe feed. A player can finish a
round and move on, and can judge a game in seconds. The costs: it rules out deeper games, and
every game needs a round that restarts cleanly.

**Any game.** A wider catalog. The costs: a long game fits poorly with swiping away, an
accidental swipe loses more progress, and a larger download slows the swipe.

**Short by default, with a way to keep playing.** A game that holds the player's attention can
open outside the feed, full screen. The cost: a second mode to design.

## Recommendation

Short loops for the first version. Whether a game can open outside the feed can wait until the
feed itself works.
